Support.

Spy Pass needs one device and at least three players.

Start a game

  1. Add one name for each player.
  2. Tap Deal and pass the device to Player 1.
  3. Each player taps their card, remembers what it says, and passes the device on.
  4. Give clues, vote on the spy, then reveal the answer.

Cards hide automatically

A revealed card hides after 3.5 seconds. You can tap it to hide it sooner. After a handoff, Spy Pass briefly ignores taps so the previous player's finger cannot reveal the next card.
